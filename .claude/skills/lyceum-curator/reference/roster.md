# Roster of candidate thinkers

Use this list so the same names do not come back run after run. Check `content/thinkers.json` first: a thinker already in the corpus is not new and may receive at most one additional card per run, for an idea they do not have yet. Years in brackets are the work to start from; the curator verifies every one of them before setting `v:true`.
